Privacy Policy

Naveen PDF Editor · Last updated 4 October 2026

In short: the PDFs you open are kept on this server only while you work with them and are deleted automatically within an hour. There are no accounts, no cookies, no advertising and no tracking, and nothing is shared with anyone else.

What we receive

  • The PDF files you open, and files you add for tools such as Merge or Insert pages. They are needed to show the pages and run the tools.
  • Passwords of protected PDFs and passwords you choose when protecting a PDF, and the certificate file and its password when you sign a PDF. These are used only for the request that needs them and are never stored or written to logs.
  • Your IP address, which every web request carries. It is used only to limit how many requests one address can send per minute (to keep the service available for everyone), and is held in memory for about a minute, not stored.

How long files are kept

  • Uploaded PDFs, and new PDFs made from page edits, are stored in a temporary folder under random names and deleted automatically 60 minutes after upload.
  • Files made by the tools (merged, split, compressed, converted, protected or signed copies, reports) are sent to your browser and deleted from the server as soon as they are sent.
  • Your file contents are never copied elsewhere, never used for any other purpose, and never shared, sold or shown to anyone else.

What stays in your browser

Some features keep data only on your own device, in your browser's storage. It is never sent to the server:

  • Recent files: copies of the last 5 PDFs you opened, so you can open them again. Use File > Clear recent files to delete them.
  • Settings such as the light / dark theme, view mode, measurement units, recent colours, saved custom markups and the author name for revisions.
  • Markups you draw are kept in the page's memory until you close it, unless you save a copy of the PDF.
  • Text recognition (OCR) runs entirely in your browser; page images are not uploaded.

Clearing your browser's site data removes all of this.

What we do not do

  • No user accounts, sign-up or login.
  • No cookies.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking, and no third-party services: every part of this site is served from this server.

Security

Connections to this site use HTTPS, so files and passwords are encrypted on the way. Files are stored under random names that cannot be guessed, only PDF files are accepted, and uploaded files are never run.

Your choices

Do not upload documents you are not allowed to share with this service. If you want a file removed before the 60 minutes are up, contact us (below) with the time you uploaded it.

Contact

For questions about this policy or your data, contact the operator of this site.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
